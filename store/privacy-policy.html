This policy explains how the Volt Runner game ("the app"), published by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME], handles your information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (unlocked levels and best times) only on your device. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app sends your level-clear count and sector completion times to Google Play Games leaderboards so you can compare times with other players. Google handles your Play Games profile and leaderboard data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The app has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
